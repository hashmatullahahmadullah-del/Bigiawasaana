Our Catering Services

Whether you are hosting a wedding, corporate lunch, film set catering, or a private backyard party, Bigi Awasaana delivers fresh, flavorful, and 100% Zabiha Halal meals that will leave your guests amazed. We handle events of all sizes across Los Angeles.
